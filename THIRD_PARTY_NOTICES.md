# Third-party notices

Runtime dependencies such as Arma 3 and CBA_A3 are not redistributed.

## Virtual JTAC

`addons/support/EPD/VirtualJTAC` started as Virtual JTAC by EPD and has been
reworked since. The original scripts stay under their author's terms.

## Models and sounds

The ordnance models in `addons/support/iskander`, `kinjal`, `tomahawk`, `X101`
and `umpk250`, `umpk500`, `umpk1500`, `umpk9000`, and the sound
`oreshnik/sounds/rok.ogg` came with the mod without a recorded source. Their
authors and licenses are to be confirmed before a Workshop release.

## Optional mods

The Drones menu uses classes from Geran-2 & Shahed-136, Geran-4 and ArmaFPV
when they are loaded. Nothing from those mods is included.
